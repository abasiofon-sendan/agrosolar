AgroSolar Technopreneurship and Innovation Centre (ASTIC)

AgroSolar Harvest is an innovative initiative under ASTIC, a British Council-funded project at the University of Uyo. We develop solar-powered agro-processing solutions to reduce post-harvest losses, improve food security, and promote clean energy in agriculture.

Our Mission, Vision & Support

Mission

To equip students and farmers with the knowledge and tools to develop solar-powered agro-processing solutions, promote green entrepreneurship, and bridge the gap between academia and industry.

Vision

To be a leading hub for sustainable agricultural innovation in Africa, driving clean energy adoption and reducing post-harvest losses.

Support

Supported by the British Council and global partners, AgroSolar provides training, mentorship, funding opportunities, and access to international expertise in green technology.
