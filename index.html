ASTIC MOTTO

Innovate * Transform * Sustain

Research | Innovation | Entrepreneurship | Technology Transfer | Sustainability

Mission

To harness renewable energy, scientific research and entrepreneurship to develop and deploy innovative technologoes that create economic opportunities, empower communities and contribute to sustainable national development.

Vision

To become a leading African centre for innovative, sustainable and commercially viable technologies that enhance agricultural productivity, reduce post-harvest losses and provide food security.

Support

Supported by the British Council and global partners, AgroSolar provides training, mentorship, funding opportunities, and access to international expertise in green technology.

Our Core Focus

ASTIC focuses on renewable energy, agricultural innovation, sustainable materials, research, technopreneurship, skills development and international collaboration.
