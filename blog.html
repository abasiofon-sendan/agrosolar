PROJECT MILESTONE

AgroSolar Harvest Prototype: Turning Solar Energy into a Practical Agricultural Solution

The AgroSolar Technopreneurship and Innovation Centre (ASTIC) has reached an important project milestone with the development of the AgroSolar Harvest prototype.

The solar-powered agricultural storage solution is designed to support the preservation of perishable produce and contribute to efforts to reduce post-harvest losses.

With the prototype now built, the project has moved into the next stage of testing, evaluation, refinement and demonstration.
