Revolutionizing Agriculture with Solar-Powered Storage

Agriculture remains the backbone of many African economies, yet post-harvest losses continue to threaten food security and farmer income. AgroSolar introduces an innovative solution — a solar-powered cold storage system designed to preserve perishable crops such as tomatoes, vegetables, and fruits without relying on unstable electricity.

This technology harnesses renewable solar energy to power a refrigeration unit that keeps harvested produce fresh for longer periods. By doing so, farmers can reduce waste, stabilize market prices, and increase their profitability.

The AgroSolar device is not just a storage system; it is a step toward sustainable agriculture. It promotes clean energy usage while addressing real-life challenges faced by rural farmers.

Training the Next Generation of Agro-Tech Innovators

At AgroSolar Technopreneurship and Innovation Centre (ASTIC), students and young entrepreneurs are trained to design, build, and maintain solar-powered agro devices. This hands-on training bridges the gap between theory and real-world application.

Participants gain practical skills in solar installation, electrical systems, and agricultural technology. The program empowers them to become job creators, innovators, and solution providers in their communities.
