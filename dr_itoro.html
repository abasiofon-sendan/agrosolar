My Profile

Dr Itoro Udoh

Dr. Itoro Esiet Udo is an Industrial Chemist, researcher, and academic currently serving as a Senior Lecturer in the Department of Chemistry, Faculty of Physical Sciences, University of Uyo, Akwa Ibom State, Nigeria. He has over a decade of experience in university teaching, applied chemical research, and innovation-driven scientific development focused on sustainable industrial solutions. He obtained his PhD in Industrial Chemistry from the University of Port Harcourt in 2017, following M.Sc. in Analytical Chemistry (2009) and a B.Sc. in Applied Chemistry (2002) both from the University of Uyo. His academic training provides strong expertise in analytical methods, industrial processes, and applied materials science.
Dr. Udo is an active member of the global scientific community. He is a Member of the American Chemical Society (ACS), an Executive Officer of the Chemical Society of Nigeria (CSN), an Associate Member of the Nigerian Institute of Science Laboratory Technology, and a certified teacher registered with the Teachers Registration Council of Nigeria. He also contributes professionally to the field of Pulp and Paper Science and Technology.
His research focuses on translating laboratory research into industry-relevant applications, integrating industrial chemistry, renewable energy systems, sustainable materials development, agro-processing technologies, and environmental sustainability. His research team work includes AgroSolar technologies, storage innovations, and eco-friendly material development aimed at improving energy efficiency, resource utilization and food security.
Dr. Udo founded the AgroSolar TechnoPreneurship and Innovation Centre (ASTIC) to promote renewable energy integration, research commercialization, and technology-driven entrepreneurship. He has led and participated in several funded research projects, including a 2025 British Council grant supporting solar-powered agro-processing innovation, a 2024 TETFund project on bentonitic clay beneficiation for drilling fluids, and an RMRDC-funded agro-waste paper production initiative.
Through teaching, mentorship, and research leadership, Dr. Udo advances innovation, capacity building, and sustainable industrial development in Nigeria and beyond.
