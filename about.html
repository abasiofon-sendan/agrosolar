About Us

We are a World-Class AgroSolar Provider

AgroSolar Harvest is an innovative initiative under the AgroSolar Technopreneurship and Innovation Centre (ASTIC), a British Council-funded project at the University of Uyo, Nigeria.

The project is a collaboration with Manchester Metropolitan University (UK), TISG Ltd, and Benue State Polytechnic. It focuses on developing solar-powered agro-processing technologies that reduce post-harvest losses, improve food security, and promote clean energy in agriculture.

Through hands-on training, innovation, and enterprise development, AgroSolar empowers students and young entrepreneurs to transform ideas into practical, sustainable solutions aligned with global development goals.
