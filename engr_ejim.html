Michael Ejim

Co-Founder and CEO, TISG

Michael Ejim is the Co-Founder and CEO of TISG (Technology Innovation & Sustainability Group), a UK deep-tech engineering company specialising in wireless signal integrity, RF engineering, artificial intelligence, advanced wireless technologies, IoT, and digital sustainability solutions.

A technology entrepreneur, digital transformation consultant, and innovation leader, Michael brings more than a decade of experience delivering complex transformation programmes across the aviation, healthcare, public sector, and research sectors. He is passionate about turning emerging technologies into practical solutions that address global sustainability challenges.

Under his leadership, TISG has developed innovations in advanced RF engineering, AI-enabled systems, ESG intelligence, and smart agriculture technologies. As Project Manager of the British Council-funded AgroSolar Technopreneurship Centre (ASTIC), Michael leads the delivery of solar-powered IoT solutions for agriculture while empowering young innovators to build sustainable solutions for their communities.

A strong advocate for collaboration between academia, industry, and government, Michael believes these partnerships are key to accelerating innovation. As Exhibition Chair of the ASSET Conference (2024–2026), he champions research, entrepreneurship, and the adoption of sustainable technology. At ASSET 2026, he delivered the keynote "AgroSolar Innovation: The Role of ASSET," which explored how technology and collaboration can accelerate climate-smart agriculture and inspire the next generation of innovators.
